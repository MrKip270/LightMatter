Before you start stargazing

Find out what’s actually
visible tonight.

Enter a place, use your location, or click the map. LightMatter combines cloud cover, light pollution, moonlight and aurora activity into a single answer — and tells you the best window to go outside.

  • Two scores. Tonight is what conditions allow right now; At its best is what the place offers on a clear, moonless night — so a cloudy night somewhere great still reads as worth a return trip.
  • Layers — the tab on the map's edge — toggles the light-pollution and aurora overlays and their opacity sliders.
  • Find a darker sky shows up under your result and suggests a nearby spot that's clear tonight, or the darkest one nearby regardless of forecast.
